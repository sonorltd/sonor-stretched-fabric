How it works

Five steps to your proposal.

Rooms and wall lengths flow in from the take-off plan and the cinema design — you choose the system and the fabric.

Rooms

Pick rooms from the plan, the cinema design, or by size. Switch each wall and ceiling on or off.

System

12 mm fabric-only track or 25 / 50 mm acoustic track with mineral-wool infill.

Fabric

Velvets, suedes and acoustically transparent weaves from the Cineca range, with colour cards.

Layout

Openings and device cut-outs on every surface, with the panel set-out drawn for each wall.

Proposal

A branded proposal PDF, the internal cut list, and the quantities pushed straight to WeQuote.
